## Understanding the 1:1 Mapping Structure

* **Local Sec # (`roman` / `section_number`)**: The exact section heading in the local Dutt text (e.g., `Section I`, `Section LXXVII`).
* **JSON Index (`index`)**: The 0-based array index within the corresponding `Book_*_narrative.json` file.
* **Canonical Sanskrit Sarga Name**: The traditional Sanskrit title assigned by classical commentators (*Govindarāja*, *Nāgojī Bhaṭṭa*) and standard editions (*Gita Press*, *Baroda Critical Edition*).
* **Thematic Title & Summary**: A precise synopsis of the events narrated in that specific local section.
* **Word Count**: The exact length of the local narrative prose for that chapter.
